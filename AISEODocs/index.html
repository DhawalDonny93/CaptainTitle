What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of optimizing your content to appear in AI-generated responses. Unlike traditional SEO which focuses on search engine rankings, AEO ensures your brand is cited and mentioned when AI platforms answer user queries.

Traditional SEO AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Optimize for search rankings Optimize for AI citations
Focus on keywords Focus on prompts and questions
Drive clicks to website Get mentioned in AI responses
Backlinks matter most Content quality and authority matter most
